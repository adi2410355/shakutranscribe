尺八譜 About This App

This application translates Western monophonic notation into traditional vertical Japanese shakuhachi notation, supporting both Chikumeisha (Kinko-ryu) hyōshi beat markings and authentic Tozan-ryu cellular metric grids.

How to Use:

  • Uniform Multi-Page Scores: Complete sequences and chants are automatically formatted into balanced 8-column pages. Use the ◀ Prev / Next Page ▶ buttons (or Left/Right keyboard arrows) in both normal and Full Screen views.
  • Select Your School & Flute: Choose between Kinko-ryu and Tozan-ryu, and select your shakuhachi size (defaulting to standard 1.8 in D).
  • Kinko-ryu vs. Tozan-ryu:
    • Kinko-ryu (Chikumeisha): Uses ● Omote-ma (right downbeat dots), ○ Ura-ma (left upbeat circles), and vertical sustain/beam lines.
    • Tozan-ryu: Uses a continuous boxed measure grid with time signature headers (4/4, 2/4, 3/4), horizontal sustain dashes (ー), left-hand eighth-note beams, and parenthetical register marks (乙) / (甲).
